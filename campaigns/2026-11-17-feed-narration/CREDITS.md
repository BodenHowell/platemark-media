# PlateMark Male 2 feature campaign

10 original PlateMark videos: seven narrated feed walkthroughs and three app detail videos using real app recordings and synthetic narration. No search or dish creation screen.

Synthetic narration generated with Chatterbox Turbo (MIT). Reference: speaker p334 from the CSTR VCTK Corpus, CC BY 4.0, Junichi Yamagishi, Christophe Veaux and Kirsten MacDonald (2019), provided through Kyutai TTS Voices. Adapted for synthetic voice conditioning.
